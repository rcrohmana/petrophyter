# Petrophyter v1.7.2 (Build 20261011)

Petrophyter v1.7.2 is a calculation-correction release. Total porosity now exists on every porosity path, Waxman-Smits and Dual-Water are solved on total porosity and reported as effective saturation, automatic Rw looks for a real water leg, pay thickness and HCPV share one depth rule, and pay averages are weighted. **Results change**: every change is listed under [Calculation changes](#calculation-changes) with its example numbers. The log tracks show real units, the run's checks are coded by severity, and sessions recognise when their source files changed.

## Calculation changes

Sessions saved by 1.7.1 and older load with a notice in the banner ("Calculations changed in 1.7.2 …"), and every well is out of date until you run the analysis. Results are never stored in a session, so nothing is changed behind your back. The corrections are not options and there is no legacy mode.

### Porosity

- **C1 Total porosity on every path.** PHIT exists wherever there is a porosity log: PHID and PHIN are combined by the new **D-N combination**, **Average** `(PHID + PHIN) / 2` by default or **RMS** `√((PHID² + PHIN²) / 2)`; RMS on gas-flagged samples whatever the choice (Gaymard-Poupon); PHID, PHIN or PHIS alone where only one exists. PHIE_DN combines the shale-corrected pair the same way. Before, PHIT was the RMS everywhere, which overstates liquid-filled porosity, and was missing on single-tool paths. **To reproduce the old PHIT, set D-N combination to RMS** (per well or per zone).
- **C4 Gas flag.** A sample is gas when `PHID − PHIN ≥ 0.02` and `Vsh ≤ 0.40` (both settable), and whenever gas correction is on, whatever the primary PHIE method. PHIE_GAS averages the corrected pair and never exceeds the sample's PHIT. A bare crossover used to flag shale and noise.
- **C5 Neutron shale term.** `PHIE_N = PHIN − Vsh × (NPHI_shale − NPHI_matrix)`; pure shale now gives 0 instead of 0.02 of porosity on a sandstone matrix.

### Water saturation

- **C2, C3 Waxman-Smits and Dual-Water on PHIT.** Both are total-porosity models and are now solved on PHIT. The total saturation is kept as `SWT_WS` / `SWT_DW`; `SW_WS` / `SW_DW` and `SW` are the **effective** saturation from the hydrocarbon volume, `BVH = min(PHIT × (1 − Swt), PHIE)`, `Swe = 1 − BVH / PHIE`. Example: Rt 20 ohm·m, PHIT 0.25, PHIE 0.225, Dual-Water → Swt 0.2162, Swe 0.1291, HCPV fraction 0.1959. Where PHIE cannot hold the model's hydrocarbon volume (inconsistent porosities or parameters), no more hydrocarbon than PHIE is reported, and the run counts and warns.
- **C6 One validity rule.** Every model gives Sw 1 where its porosity is ≤ 0.001 and RT is valid (Indonesian, Waxman-Smits and Dual-Water gave no Sw), and limits Vsh to 0–1 (Simandoux did not).
- **C14 Bounds are counted.** Sw stays limited to 0–1, but every limited sample is counted per model and zone, shown in Diagnostics, with a warning when more than 10 % of a zone's clean samples sit at the upper bound ("check Rw, m, n or Rt").
- **Waxman-Smits with Qv 0** (clean sand) runs; it used to abort with "qv must contain finite values greater than 0".

### Rw, Rsh, shale

- **C8 Automatic Rw needs a water leg.** Rw is the median Rwa of the samples that are water-like against the 5th-percentile seed (Sw ≥ 0.8), with at least 10 of them; the run says whether it found a water leg, saw no water/hydrocarbon contrast (warning), or found no water (entered Rw used). Example: with 10 % water the estimate is 0.050 ohm·m; before it was 0.80, the hydrocarbon column. Automatic Rsh needs 10 shale samples. Both warn when their limits apply.
- **C9 Zone GR baseline.** An automatic zone baseline keeps the zone's P95 as GRmax only when the zone contains shale (it reaches 75 % of the well's GR range); otherwise GRmax is the well's, with the zone note "zone has no shale; GRmax from the well". A clean sand with GR 30–60 in a 20–120 well gets a mean Vsh of about 0.15 instead of 0.48.
- **C11 Primary Vsh.** `VSH` comes from the **Primary Vsh** method (default: the first selected, Linear) instead of the largest of the selected methods.

### Pay, HCPV, Swirr, permeability

- **C10 One thickness rule.** Gross, net reservoir, net pay and HCPV use the same per-sample thickness, with gaps found against the local depth step. A 100 ft section at 0.5 ft in a 0.1524 ft log counted 30.5 ft of HCPV before; it now counts 100 ft for both pay and HCPV.
- **C12 Weighted pay averages.** PHIE and Vsh in pay are thickness weighted; Sw is pore-volume weighted (`ΣPHIE × Sw × dz / ΣPHIE × dz`). Example: PHIE 0.30 / 0.10 with Sw 0.2 / 0.6 → 0.30 (0.40 as a plain mean). The exported field Sw is weighted the same way.
- **C7 Swirr order.** Hierarchical Swirr is `clip(min(estimate, Sw), 0.05, 0.60)`: never below 0.05.
- **C15 The selected Swirr method feeds permeability.** Clean Zone, Statistical and Buckles Number now set `SWIRR`; where the method has no value the sample takes Buckles, counted. All Methods keeps Buckles and says so ("All methods (Buckles used)").
- **C13 Permeability calibration.** C and P are fitted with Q held at the entered value (the three are not identifiable together with Buckles Swirr), and the fit uses the same Buckles Swirr as the run (porosity floored at 0.01, Swirr 0.05–0.5). Before, at porosity 0.025 the applied permeability was 2.56 × the fitted model. **Apply** warns when the Swirr method or Buckles k differs from the fit.

- The porosity changes (C1, C5) drive most of the differences: the Average combination gives a lower PHIT than RMS, and the neutron shale term lowers PHIE_N and PHIE_DN.
- The gas case is synthetic (Vsh around 0.45 with a constant Qv): the entered Qv is too low for that much shale, so Waxman-Smits' total hydrocarbon volume does not fit in PHIE on any sample; Sw is 0 and the run warns that the hydrocarbon volume was limited to PHIE in 50 of 50 samples. Its automatic Rsh now falls back to the entered 5 ohm·m (only 9 shale samples; it was 50, at the limit).
- Two new reference cases lock the rules: **Dual-Water primary** (net pay 56.95 ft, HCPV 7.071, avg Sw 0.164) and **mixed depth step** (0.5 ft over 0.1524 ft: net pay 32.42 ft, HCPV 4.449, avg Sw 0.246).

## Interface

- **Log tracks in real units.** Both log engines read one track definition: real-value axes (log decades on resistivity and permeability), axis labels and a legend per track in Interactive, GR on the GR / Vsh track in Classic, porosity 0.5–0. The Sw track draws the primary model as "Sw − <model> (primary)" and the others dashed. A Wyllie-Rose-only run shows its permeability.
- **Histograms and table follow the primary.** The Sw histogram shows `SW` with a selector over every model; the permeability histogram has a selector; the results table shows `SW`, the `SW_*` and `SWT_*` curves, PHIT and PERM_WR.
- **Plot controls work.** The HCPV mode, the tops toggle and the crossplot depth sync act on both engines; controls that cannot act are disabled with a tooltip.
- **Honest export preview.** The preview says what it shows ("showing 500 of 4,812 rows"), and the depth interval (All depths or Selected interval) applies to the CSV, Excel and LAS export, and is written in the provenance.
- **Settings for the corrections.** **D-N combination** (Average / RMS) on the Porosity Method page, **Crossover min** and **Gas Vsh max** under Gas Correction (both per well and per zone), and **Primary Vsh** on the VShale page. Calculate Rw/Rsh shows how many water-leg and shale samples the estimate rests on and its warnings; the Permeability page names the source of its result and shows calibration failures, limited plugs and the Swirr mismatch warning under it, without removing **Apply**. The temperature readout turns to warning style when the header BHT and TD cannot give a gradient.
- **Diagnostics.** **Sw Solver and Porosity Checks** counts, per Sw model and zone, the samples with no porosity, at the upper and lower bound, with the hydrocarbon volume capped to PHIE, with PHIE above PHIT, and the solver failures, with cells above their limit in warning style. It names the Swirr method used and its floor, Sw, 0.60 and fallback counts, and lists zones whose GR baseline found no shale. Core samples beside a log gap read "N core samples beside a log gap excluded".
- **Run issues by severity.** Every run message carries a code from a closed list, a severity and a target. Counts below their limit are notes, not warnings, so a normal run shows a success banner. **Go to** selects the Rw or Rsh field when the issue is about one of them. Run summaries keep the plain warning list for older readers.

## Sessions and provenance

- **Source fingerprints.** Loaded files are hashed (SHA-256 and size). A session restored with a changed file says so ("a.las changed since the session was saved"); a missing file opens **Locate a.las…**, and the folder you pick is tried first for the other missing files. Older sessions restore as before.
- **Run manifest.** The provenance in Excel, LAS and CSV records the source fingerprints, the merge recipe, the tops and core hashes, and the numpy, scipy, pandas and lasio versions.

## Quality

- Hand-worked textbook values for every Vsh, porosity, Sw, Swirr and permeability formula, and property tests (Hypothesis) on the Sw models.
- The test suite has 1320 tests.

## Known issues

- The window title shows the active well, not the session file name.
- The Interactive log engine is verified on axes and items in automated tests; its drawing is checked by hand on a real display.
- The hint that a deviated well was assumed vertical rarely appears, because the LAS parser does not read inclination or deviation header entries. Map a TVD curve for deviated wells.

## Installation

1. Download `Petrophyter_Setup_1.7.2_Build20261011.exe` from **Assets**.
2. Optionally verify it using `SHA256SUMS.txt`:

   ```powershell
   Get-FileHash .\Petrophyter_Setup_1.7.2_Build20261011.exe -Algorithm SHA256
   ```

3. Run the installer and launch **Petrophyter** from the Start Menu. Installing over v1.7.1 upgrades it in place.

> Windows SmartScreen may appear because the installer is unsigned. Select **More info → Run anyway** only after confirming the SHA-256 hash.
