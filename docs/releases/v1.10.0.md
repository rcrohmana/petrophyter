# Petrophyter v1.10.0 (Build 20261021)

Petrophyter v1.10.0 adds saturation-height modelling. Laboratory capillary pressure curves are fitted per rock type with the Leverett J-function, and a free water level per well and zone turns the height above it into a second water saturation, `SW_SHF`, that you can compare with the resistivity Sw or use for net pay and HCPV. It is optional: **until you enable it, results, exports and saved sessions are the same as in v1.9.0.**

## New

- **Pc data import.** **File → Open Pc Data…** (the active well) and **File → Open Pc Data (Multi-Well)…** (a file with a well column, assigned to loaded wells in the import dialog). Mercury-injection, porous-plate and centrifuge curves; Pc in psi, kPa, MPa, bar or atm (asked for when the header names none); the fluid system (air-brine, mercury-air or oil-brine) asked for when the file lacks it; porosity and permeability completed from the well's core data when the file has none. Excluded rows are counted with their reason. Loading Pc data changes no result.
- **Saturation-Height tab** (after Rock Typing).
  - **Fluids and tensions.** Water, oil and gas densities and the interfacial tension times cosine of the contact angle of each system, marked as defaults until you confirm or edit them.
  - **Fit.** Power law or Brooks-Corey, one curve per rock type (or one curve for all samples when rock typing is off or the rock types are fitted per zone), fitted to the core Pc data of all wells in the background. Weighting by sample or by point, a minimum Pc for closure, the permeability and porosity columns used for J, and an optional pooled curve for a rock type without its own. Plots of J against Sw, Sw against height and the profile around the free water level, and a table of the fits with their basis and status. **Apply fit** writes them to the project.
  - **Fit from logs.** A fit to the log Sw above the contacts, shown as log-derived. It is a cross-check only.
  - **Contacts per well.** A default free water level and one per zone, with the fluid and an optional water density per zone (blank = the project value), in TVD or TVDSS with a typed kelly bushing elevation (the LAS header values are only a hint). **Estimate FWL** proposes a contact where the model best matches the log Sw, or says it is not identifiable; **Accept** stores it.
  - **Misfit.** For the well, each zone and each rock type: samples, bias, RMSE, MAE and R2 of `SW` against `SW_SHF` over net reservoir above the free water level.
  - **Compute saturation-height Sw on Run.** Adds `SW_SHF`, `HAFWL` (height above the free water level, ft) and `SW_SHF_RESID` (`SW − SW_SHF`) to the results.
  - **Use SHF Sw for pay and HCPV.** Net pay, N/G, average Sw and HCPV read `SW_PAY` (the saturation-height Sw where it exists, else `SW`). Available only with core Pc fits.
- **Log display.** `SW_SHF` is drawn dashed on the Sw track as **Sw SHF** (and dotted as **Sw SHF (pay)** when it is used for pay), a **Sw − Sw SHF** residual track follows the Rock Type track, and the interactive readout shows the height above the free water level. Both engines draw them.
- **Uncertainty.** With SHF Sw used for pay, the samples that have it keep it in every Monte Carlo realisation; the contact and the fit parameters are not varied, and the Uncertainty tab and sheet say so. Without it, the Monte Carlo result is unchanged.
- **Exports.** `SW_SHF`, `HAFWL`, `SW_SHF_RESID` and, when used, `SW_PAY` in the CSV, Excel and LAS results, and an **SHF** sheet in the Excel workbook with the misfit by well, zone and rock type, the contacts and densities used, and the sources of permeability and porosity.

## Sessions

- A session is saved as format **2.4** only when the project holds saturation-height content (saturation-height modelling switched on, an applied fit, Pc data or a contact). It adds the settings, the fits, each well's Pc file and import record, and its contacts. Pc files are read again from their saved paths when the session is opened. Any other session is saved as 2.3, 2.2 or 2.1 as before.
- **A 2.4 session opens in v1.9.x and older without the saturation-height data**, and saving it again there drops it.
- An uncertainty setup with every distribution switched off is now saved with the session, so a changed N, seed, sampling or correlation is not lost.

## Calculation changes

- None to existing results; every reference case is unchanged relative to v1.9.0. Two new reference cases cover a core-Pc fit used for pay and HCPV, and a log-derived fit whose use for pay is refused.

## Known issues

- Log-derived fits are a cross-check only: they are fitted to the resistivity Sw, so their residual says how well `SW` follows height, not whether `SW` is right. SHF Sw cannot be used for pay with them.
- The free water level is one depth per well or zone. There is no pressure-gradient free water level (different fluid gradients), and no closure correction beyond the minimum Pc you set.
- When the rock-type definitions are fitted per zone, or rock typing is off, one fit is used for all samples.
- Heights use a TVD curve, a TVD depth index, or measured depth assumed vertical. A TVD computed from a deviation survey is not supported.
- The permeability used for J can come from the resistivity Sw (through Swirr), and the log rock types are classified from the same permeability, so `SW_SHF_RESID` can look smaller than the real disagreement. Core permeability is independent.
- The water and hydrocarbon densities and the interfacial tensions are typical defaults; confirm them for your field.

## Installation

1. Download `Petrophyter_Setup_1.10.0_Build20261021.exe` from **Assets**.
2. Optionally verify it using `SHA256SUMS.txt`:

   ```powershell
   Get-FileHash .\Petrophyter_Setup_1.10.0_Build20261021.exe -Algorithm SHA256
   ```

3. Run the installer and launch **Petrophyter** from the Start Menu. Installing over v1.9.0 upgrades it in place.

> Windows SmartScreen may appear because the installer is unsigned. Select **More info → Run anyway** only after confirming the SHA-256 hash.
